How do ultra-fine particles differ from particle-free technology?

Very small particles are not the same as no particles. Visual clarity alone does not establish particle size or uniform tissue distribution. This page follows the brand-provided ultra-fine-particle description and distinguishes it from particle-free PCL research.

Rat model · particle-free technology

Hong JY et al.
2021

Dermatol Ther. 34(2):e14770.

Evaluates safety, degradation and neocollagenesis of a particle-free PCL formulation in rats. Its technology differs from this product’s ultra-fine-particle description.

Mouse model · thread study

Cho SW et al.
2021

J Cosmet Dermatol. 20(9):2743–2749.

05 / BUYER FAQ

Questions buyers ask first.

How should efficacy and duration be assessed?

Review material literature separately from product-specific tests and clinical data. The supplied product’s authorized use and current IFU take priority; outcomes and duration can vary by individual and treatment conditions.

How do I ask about MOQ, price and samples?

Share your target country, company or clinic, quantity and timing. Availability, quotations and sample terms are confirmed individually.

Which documents should I request?

Start with current specifications, labeling and IFU, then ask about registration and batch-test documentation required for your market. Check that each document applies to the actual product and lot.

What is needed for overseas distribution?

Review target-market registration, import requirements and distribution qualifications. Publication of this page does not establish authorization to sell in a particular country.
