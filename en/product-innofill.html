PLLA VS PDLLA

Same Lactic Acid Family,
Different Molecular Architecture

Differences in stereochemical arrangement and crystallinity can influence water penetration, hydrolysis, and the time course of tissue response.

PLLAPDLLA
Stereochemical StructurePredominantly regular L-form arrangementMixed D/L-form arrangement
CrystallinitySemi-crystallineAmorphous
Degradation RateRelatively gradual hydrolysisPotentially faster hydrolysis
Tissue ResponseFavors a more sustained stimulation profileTends toward an earlier-phase response
Collagen DurabilityGeared toward long-term structural remodelingMore focused on mid-term tissue improvement

Actual clinical performance is also influenced by molecular weight, particle size and morphology, reconstitution concentration, injected volume, and treatment plane.
