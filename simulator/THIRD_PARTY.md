# Third-party components and information

The build retrieves MediaPipe Tasks Vision 0.10.21 from the npm CDN and the Google Face Landmarker float16 version 1 model. The distributable asset manifest records source URLs, sizes and SHA-256 hashes of the exact downloaded bytes. These hashes record the build; they do not replace independently reviewed/pinned supply-chain integrity.
