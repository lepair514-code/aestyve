# HA collection studio

The Korean, English and Chinese detail pages are generated by
`python3 tools/build-ha-studio.py`. Do not run the superseded
`build-ha-pages.py` to update these pages. Styling and interaction are in
`assets/ha-studio.css` and `assets/ha-studio.js`.

## Source assets

- The supplied `엠블럼흰색.png` is copied without alteration to
  `assets/ha-studio/emblem-white.png` and used behind the home/detail products.
- `알파.png`, `베타.png`, `감마.png`: supplied line symbols, copied unchanged.
- `Photoroom_20260825_233140.JPG`, `233046.JPG`, `233213.JPG`: supplied gel
  texture photographs, copied unchanged as gel-1/2/3.jpg. These photographs
  do not represent test-result images or a verified product-to-texture mapping.
- Existing ha_*_pack.webp and ha_*_syringe.webp are the real packaging and
  syringe photographs. Their bytes are unchanged. CSS/SVG silhouette clipping
  removes the surrounding white photo mat in the rendered composition while
  preserving the actual printed labels. Image aspect ratios are preserved.
- SEM and MRI JPEGs are extracted original embedded images from the supplied
  `AESTYVE_Catalog_Preview.pdf` pages 5 and 10; no scientific images or curves
  were generated. MRI observation intervals show the original complete strips.
- The same approved 12-page catalog is available as
  `AESTYVE_HA_Collection_2026_KO.pdf`, with lossless PDF resource cleanup only.
- The supplied 360° videos and glass-dashboard image inform the motion,
  layered composition and restrained lighting; reference videos are not embedded.

## Data boundaries

24 mg/mL HA, 1.1 mL volume, 3 mg/mL (0.3%) lidocaine and the individual
needle/cannula configurations follow Aestyve carton labels (P01).
Fine/Deep/Volume G′ ranges, cohesion lengths and Deep/27G extrusion mean
are manufacturer formulation data (T01/T02); no unverified mapping to
Aestyve clinical outcomes is asserted. Range bars are true min–max spans
on a 0–300 Pa axis, not invented time series. Raw-material criteria are
separate from final-product results. MRI is preclinical observation, not
human duration/safety evidence. Source limitations appear next to the data.

## Interaction and accessibility

Drag the product area horizontally to select a line and reveal composition.
Product buttons, previous/next controls and arrow keys provide alternatives.
Gel texture, rheology and MRI panels support drag, keyboard and buttons.
Vertical touch scrolling remains native until horizontal intent is established.
Reduced motion removes easing; animation stops outside the viewport and when
the tab is hidden. No continuously running decorative render loop is used.

Home hero: the existing 3D carton implementation is retained; only background
lighting and the supplied emblem replace the previous decorative wordmark.
